About Cosmos

A solar system you can scrub through time, from 1801 to 2198. Positions are computed in your browser, and facts come from NASA.

Positions
astronomy-engine, with illustrative orbits for a few small moons, labelled as such.
Facts
NASA NSSDC Planetary Fact Sheet, version of 18 March 2025.
Missions
Spacecraft, comets and asteroids, where shown, are sampled offline from NASA JPL Horizons. Nothing is requested from JPL while you browse.
Surfaces
NASA and USGS maps where shown. Anything else is procedural, and the body's plate says so.
Dispatch
Astronomy Picture of the Day, near-Earth objects and solar flares from api.nasa.gov.
Code
Built with Three.js and astronomy-engine, both MIT licensed. Type is IBM Plex Sans, IBM Plex Mono and Instrument Serif, under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.
